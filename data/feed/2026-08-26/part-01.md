TEXT: Tip: Pick your model and effort at the top of a session. 

Switching models mid-session forces a full uncached re-read of your entire context. The KV cache is tied to specific weights - it can't transfer. Effort level and fast mode work the same way.

It's a one-turn tax that scales with depth. Okay to switch on the first few turns but expensive later on.
QUOTED @Teknium: Just FYI. If you are switching models in a session all the time - you are doing it wrong.

Every time you switch, your entire prompt cache is invalidated on the new model you switch to, and you have to repay the full input tokens price for all of it.

Stop doing this unless those models are free.

This is not a hermes thing - this is a fundamentals of inference thing.
