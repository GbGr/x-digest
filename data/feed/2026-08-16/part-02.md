TEXT: Intuition: Compaction is agentic recurrence (RNNs), whereas recursion (RLMs) is agentic attention.

Recurrence maintains a constant-size state at all times by compressing the past.

Attention keeps all context fully represented and then decides how to re-process it for each step.
[->] In reality, recursion is quite a bit more powerful than attention, but the analogy is still useful. RLMs can, say, easily express compaction in a way attention can't do for recurrence. The reverse simulation (of recursion by compaction) requires heavy assumptions.
