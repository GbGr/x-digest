Despite the hype, public examples of true self-improving loops are few and far between - I looked far and wide, found 9 use cases, only two with verifier grounded in real world data.

In short: a self-improving loop is only as good as its verifier, and a verifier is only as good as its steady stream of real-world data.

But because building these verifiers is hard, slow, and complicated, most AI engineering is trying to side-step them - this is also where the moat / opportunity lives.
